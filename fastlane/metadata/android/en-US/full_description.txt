Knowledge Quiz brings 19 quiz games into one app. Find countries by tapping the map, learn Türkiye’s 81 provinces and their plate codes, explore the periodic table and the planets, get to know the animal kingdom and put events on the timeline. Every question is built from open data, and a short explanation follows each answer. Two players can duel face to face on one phone.

• Mixed and fast: General Knowledge, Which Is More?, True or Made Up?, Estimation Master, Put Them in Order, Quiz Duel, What Did People Say?, Quiz Ladder
• World, Türkiye and history: World Map Quiz, Provinces and Plates, History and Dates, World Kitchen, Art and Literature, Country Silhouette
• Science and nature: Elements and Science, Animals and Nature, Space and Astronomy, Mind Reader Genie

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; every question is built from data and checked to have exactly one right answer.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• 50:50, Skip and +Time helpers, plus a short explanation after every answer.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
